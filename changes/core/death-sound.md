# OPTIONAL DEATH SOUND

For players:
- Play the included local sound once when your character dies; feign death and party members stay silent.
- Turn the sound off under Fun in Settings.

Please check:
- Confirm one sound on death, no sound with the setting off, and no repeat from duplicate events.
- Confirm feign death does not trigger the sound.
